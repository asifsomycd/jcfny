Why Choose JCF? Because...


We take pride in our reputation for prudent management, transparency and personalized service, as well as our significant community impact. With JCF, your giving will be more organized--no need to track receipts. In addition, JCF reinvests in the Jewish community with a $2 million annual donation to UJA-Federation of NY and a Special Gifts Fund that supports wide-ranging community causes. Learn more about our impact here.

4 Steps to Open Your Fund

  1. Review our Policies & Procedures and fee structure.

  2. Fill out your application online. (8-10 minutes)

  3. Make your contribution of $5,000 or more in cash, appreciated securities or other assets.

  4. Review our Investment Performance Chart and complete a Fund Investment Recommendation Form.
